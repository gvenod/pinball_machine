## Global System Edge Cases & Safety Requirements

* **Power Loss Recovery:** Power loss during any active state must return the penny upon reboot, clear stale session telemetry, and reset the machine to `STATE_IDLE`.
* **Player Inactivity:** If a player inserts a penny but becomes inactive (fails to load or launch the ball within 60 seconds), automatically refund the penny and transition back to `STATE_IDLE`.
* **Missing Ball During Gameplay:** If no ball is detected in play or hits the drain, the 60-second gameplay watchdog timer terminates the round cleanly and moves to `STATE_GAMEOVER`.

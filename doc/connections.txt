WS2812B strip
• Connect A2 to the DIN pin on the WS2812B strip through a 330 Ω – 470 Ω resistor to protect the first LED from voltage spikes.
• Power (5V & GND): Power the WS2812B strip from an external 5V power supply (each RGB LED draws up to ~60 mA at full white; 30 LEDs ≈ 1.8 A, which exceeds the Arduino's onboard 5V regulator). Connect the external 5V supply GND to the Arduino GND so they share a common ground, and place a 1000 µF capacitor across the strip's 5V and GND terminals.


Piezo Sounder
• Passive Piezo (2-pin): Connect the positive (+) pin to A1 (with a 100 Ω – 220 Ω resistor in series to protect the microcontroller pin from capacitive spikes) and the negative (-) pin to GND.


Elecrow CrowPanel 7.0" HMI (ESP32-S3, 800×480) 
• dedicated 5V 2A supply via its USB-C or UART0 5V power pin (do not draw display backlight power from the UNO's 5V pin)
• 3-wire UART serial link between the two boards
    • UNO R4 TX (Pin D1 / Serial1) → Voltage Divider (1kΩ + 2kΩ to drop 5V to 3.3V) → CrowPanel UART0 RX (GPIO 44)
    • UNO R4 RX (Pin D0 / Serial1) ← CrowPanel UART0 TX (GPIO 43) (direct 3.3V-to-5V is safe)
    • UNO R4 GND ← CrowPanel GND
